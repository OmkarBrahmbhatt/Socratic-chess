About 45 minutes · on your own

Help us test two ways to learn chess tactics

You'll solve chess puzzles in three short parts. In practice, one coach asks questions and another shows an answer.

What you'll do

1

Warm-up check

10 puzzles, no hints

2

Practice

12 puzzles: 6 question-based and 6 answer-based

3

Final check

10 puzzles, no hints

Fork and pin themes are balanced across the checks. A short slider check follows each half of practice.

Before you start

  • The puzzles come from the Lichess open puzzle database. We match them to your level using the rating you tell us. Ratings from other sites and federations are converted approximately.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing this page.
  • We do not ask for your name. Your rating answers, moves, timing, and survey answers are stored under a random player code in the study database.
