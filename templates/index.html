About 45 minutes · on your own

Help us test two ways to learn chess tactics

You'll solve chess puzzles in three short parts. In practice, one coach asks questions and another shows an answer.

What you'll do

1

Warm-up check

10 puzzles, no hints

2

Practice

12 puzzles: 6 question-based and 6 answer-based

3

Final check

10 puzzles, no hints

Fork and pin themes are balanced across the checks. A short slider check follows each half of practice.

Before you start

  • This is a study prototype. The current chess positions and solutions are demo placeholders, not research puzzles.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing this page.
  • We do not ask for your name. Your rating range, moves, timing, and answers are stored under a random player code in the study database.
